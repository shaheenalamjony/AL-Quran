# Al Quran Web App

A modern, feature-rich, and beautiful Al Quran web application focused on providing a premium reading and listening experience. Built with a responsive design, dynamic theming, and offline-first preferences.

##  Features

### 📖 Reading Experience
- **Complete Quran:** Access all 114 Surahs with clear Arabic text (Amiri font).
- **Juz Navigation:** Browse by Juz (1-30) for traditional reading flow.
- **Multi-Language Translations:** Read translations in English (Sahih International), Bengali, Urdu, Hindi, Indonesian, and more.
- **Transliteration:** Toggle transliteration to help with pronunciation.
- **Tafsir:** Access detailed verse commentary (Tafsir) for deeper understanding.
- **Customizable Display:** Adjust font sizes for Arabic and Translations independently. Toggle specific text elements on/off.

###  Daily Hadith
- **Hadith of the Day:** Read authentic Hadiths from Sahih Al-Bukhari.
- **Multi-Language Support:** Toggle between English and Bengali (বাংলা) translations.
- **Save Hadiths:** Bookmark your favorite Hadiths for later reference.
- **Read Next:** Navigate through Hadiths with the "Read Next" button.
- **Daily Reading Goal:** Track your daily Hadith reading habit with visual progress.

###  Audio Player
- **Verse-by-Verse Playback:** Listen to high-quality recitations synced with the text.
- **Multiple Reciters:** Choose from world-renowned reciters like Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Basit, Sudais, and more.
- **Playback Controls:** Play, Pause, Next/Prev Ayah, and Repeat functionality.
- **Sleep Timer:** Set a timer (5m, 10m, 1h, etc.) to automatically stop playback.
- **Playback Speed:** Adjust audio speed (0.5x to 2x).

###  Customization & UI
- **Dynamic Theming:** Choose from 7+ beautiful color themes (Teal, Blue, Purple, Orange, etc.) that instantly update the entire app.
- **Dark Mode:** Fully supported dark mode for comfortable night reading.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices.
- **Settings Modal:** Centralized hub for managing all your display and reading preferences.

###  Analytics & Progress
- **Reading Stats:** Track your total reading time, verses read, and daily consistency.
- **Weekly Charts:** Visualize your activity over the last 7 days.
- **Hadith Analytics:** Track your Hadith reading streak with a monthly heatmap.
- **Heatmap:** See which Surahs you have completed.
- **Streaks:** Maintain a daily reading streak to stay motivated.

###  Prayer Times
- **Accurate Salah Times:** Get prayer times based on your specific location (City & Country).
- **Auto-Location:** Select your country and city from a comprehensive list.

###  Ramadan Calendar
- **Ramadan 2026 Countdown:** Live countdown banner to the blessed month.
- **Sehri & Iftar Times:** View daily timings for 30 days of Ramadan.
- **Daily Fazilat:** Read the spiritual virtues of each Ramadan day in English and Bengali.

###  Bookmarks & Favorites
- **Save Verses:** Quickly bookmark any Ayah to return to it later.
- **Save Hadiths:** Bookmark Hadiths with a single click.
- **Organized Tabs:** View verses and hadiths in separate tabs within the Bookmarks modal.
- **Quick Navigation:** Jump directly to any bookmarked verse.

##  Technologies Used
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Frameworks:** Bootstrap 5 (UI), jQuery (DOM Manipulation)
- **Libraries:** Select2 (Enhanced Dropdowns), Font Awesome (Icons), Chart.js (Analytics Charts).
- **APIs:**
    - [Al Quran Cloud API](https://alquran.cloud/api) - Surah data, Text, and Audio.
    - [Hadith API](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) - Hadith data in multiple languages.
    - [Aladhan API](https://aladhan.com/prayer-times-api) - Prayer Times.

##  How to Run
1.  **Download** the project files.
2.  Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3.  **No installation required!** The app runs directly in the browser.
    *   *Note: An internet connection is required to fetch Surah data, Hadiths, and Audio.*

##  Local Storage
This app uses your browser's Local Storage to save:
- Your theme and dark mode preferences.
- Last read position (Surah & Ayah).
- Bookmarks for verses and hadiths.
- Analytics data and reading streaks.
- Location settings for prayer times.
- Font sizes and translation choices.
- Hadith reading progress and daily goals.
